# Dashboard Documentation

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard built with Power BI analyzing 100K+ Olist Brazilian e-commerce orders.

## 🎯 Key Visualizations

### KPI Cards
- **Total Revenue:** ₹14.14M
- **Average Rating:** 4.03/5
- **Total Orders:** 97,917

### Charts & Insights

**1. Order Status Breakdown**
- Delivered: 97.89%
- Shipped: 0.99%
- Canceled: 0.12%

**2. Revenue by Product Category**
- Health & Beauty: Top 1
- Watches & Gifts: Top 2
- Bed & Bath: Top 3

**3. Delivery Impact on Satisfaction (Critical)**
- On-Time: 4.0/5 rating
- Late: 2.5/5 rating
- **Gap: 43% drop in satisfaction**

**4. Payment Method Distribution**
- Credit Card: 74%
- Boleto: 19%
- Others: 7%

**5. Monthly Order Trends**
- Peak: November 2017 (+51.6% MoM)
- Reason: Black Friday seasonality
- 2018: Stabilization then decline

## 🎨 Design Elements
- **Theme:** Purple professional
- **Interactive Features:** Year slicer (2016-2018)
- **All charts respond to year selection**

## 🔑 How to Use
1. Download `.pbix` file from dashboard folder
2. Open in Power BI Desktop
3. Use year slicer to filter by year
4. Hover over charts for detailed tooltips

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Improve delivery logistics (major satisfaction driver)
- Diversify payment methods (reduce credit card risk)
- Focus on customer retention (<5% repeat rate)
- Plan for seasonal peaks (November)
